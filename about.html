About me

Hi, I’m Núria

I’m a UX Lead, AI product design strategist and certified workshop facilitator. I lead UX for digital products and build the practice behind them.

I’ve designed across education, media and business software, for companies like IU International University, Sky Deutschland, Audi, BMW, Giesecke+Devrient, EADS, Vorwerk, Bulthaup and DocuWare.

Alongside my industry work, I teach Human–Computer Interaction at Universitat Oberta de Catalunya, write about AI product design and decision-making frameworks, lead a UX book club, and mentor designers.

How I lead

Building the practice

I turn design from a service into a practice: one shared system instead of scattered files, discovery as a habit, and principles the team decides with.

Leading people

I give direction and feedback, help designers grow, and let them own their work. Experienced in functional and disciplinary leadership and in hiring designers.

From user insight to roadmap

With product and engineering, we tie UX to business goals, map journeys to opportunities, and agree on the priorities that go into the roadmap and OKRs.

Growing designers

I mentor designers inside and outside my team and run a UX book club. Seeing people grow is the part of leading I care about most.
